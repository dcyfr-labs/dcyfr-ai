## Tiers

The listing at [github.com/sponsors/dcyfr](https://github.com/sponsors/dcyfr) is the source of truth. This table mirrors it.

| Tier      | Monthly | What you get                                                                                                           |
| --------- | ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Supporter | $5      | Your name here and in the README, and a short monthly build note: what shipped, what broke, what is next.               |
| Builder   | $25     | Everything in Supporter, pre-release builds, access to the private early-access repository, and first triage on issues. |
| Team      | $100    | Your logo here and on [dcyfr.ai/sponsors](https://www.dcyfr.ai/sponsors), priority triage for your team's issues, and a 30-minute call each quarter. |
| Partner   | $500    | Top logo placement, one hour of office hours a month, and a seat at the roadmap.                                        |

## How each benefit reaches you

- **Name or logo.** Added to this file and the README within a week of the sponsorship starting. Reply to the welcome message if you would like a different name, a link, or no listing at all.
- **Monthly build note.** Sent as a GitHub Sponsors update, so it arrives at the email address on your GitHub account.
- **Early-access repository.** Builder and above receive a GitHub invitation to the private repository. Pre-release builds are published there first.
- **Priority triage.** File the issue on the public repository as usual. Issues from Builder, Team and Partner sponsors are read first.
- **Calls and office hours.** The welcome message for Team and Partner carries a booking link.
